## Content notes

- `hero.jpg`, `terraces.jpg`, `auth.jpg`: Inca agricultural terraces at Moray,
  Pisac and Chinchero (Cusco, Peru).
- `potato-field.jpg`: flowering potato crop, Puno region, Peru.
- `quinoa.jpg`: red quinoa panicles on Isla del Sol, Lake Titicaca, Bolivia.
- `farmer.jpg`: potato sowing with an ox team, Bolivia; people appear only at a
  distance.
- `altiplano.jpg`: farmed shore of Lake Titicaca at Luquina, Capachica
  peninsula, Puno, Peru.
- `harvest.jpg`: potato harvest under *ayni* (reciprocal labour), Bolivian
  Altiplano; faces are turned away or shaded.
- `leaf.jpg`: potato leaf with a late blight (*Phytophthora infestans*) lesion
  and sporulation.
- `market.jpg`: native potato varieties at the Mercado Modelo, Huancayo, Peru.
- `soil.jpg`: crumb structure of a soil core under crop residue.
- `sensor.jpg`: ESP32 DevKit board of the type used by the AgroYachay sensing node.

When an image is shown on a public page, the attribution above should be
reachable from that page, for example through a "Photo credits" link in the
footer.

### Content notes (crops)

- `potato`: purple potato flowers in a field at Concepción, Junín, Peru.
- `quinoa`: rows of red, purple and yellow quinoa varieties near Uyuni, Bolivia (3800 m).
- `maize`: cobs of native Peruvian maize varieties (harvested product).
- `faba_bean`: faba bean plant with green pods, Copacabana, Bolivia.
- `oca`: harvested yellow, pink and black oca tubers (Peruvian varieties).
- `canihua`: red and yellow cañihua plants in front of a barley plot, Altiplano near Lake Titicaca; adobe houses in the distance.
- `barley`: barley ears against the sky, Concepción, Junín, Peru.
- `wheat`: ripe wheat field with mountains, Tarabuco, Chuquisaca, Bolivia.
- `other`: small-plot mixed chacra with potato rows and valley terraces, Ayaviri, Yauyos, Lima region, Peru.
